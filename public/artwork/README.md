# Artwork provenance

These three WebP backgrounds are custom AI-generated fan illustrations made for this project with OpenAI ImageGen on 2026-10-23. They are optimized crops of the generated source files; no anime episodes, promotional images, or fan-art websites were scraped.

The current exports use WebP quality 95 at a presentation size of 1920×1080. Native generated sources were 1672×941 (One Piece) and 1983×793 (the other two, cropped for composition). The presentation dimensions do not imply additional native detail or 4K source art.

The images depict characters and settings associated with One Piece, Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba, and Attack on Titan. Those characters, series names, and related marks belong to their respective rights holders. This is an unofficial fan project and is not endorsed by the rights holders. ## Additional scenes and portraits

The default presentation now uses the original illustrations again, with six additional scene illustrations and six individual character portraits generated with OpenAI ImageGen on 2026-10-23. The original artwork was supplied as a visual reference to keep the palette and anime rendering consistent.

- `scenes/*.webp`: six distinct locations, native 1672×941; WebP quality 93, no upscaling.
- `characters/*.webp`: Luffy, Zoro, Tanjiro, Nezuko, Levi and Mikasa, native 1024×1536; WebP quality 93.
- `manifest.json`: file dimensions, bytes, generator and original generated source filenames for all 12 new files.

These are newly generated fan illustrations, not official anime frames, scans, promotional art or original production character sheets. The interactive artwork view has bounded panning, zoom and gentle parallax. Full walking and 360-degree model viewing remain available in the separate 3D presentation.
